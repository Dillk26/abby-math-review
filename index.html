Quick Study Guide: How to Solve Every Quiz Question

1. Look for "Hidden" Congruent Parts (Part a)
  • Reflexive Property: If two triangles share a side, that side is congruent to itself (e.g., NM ≅ MN).
  • Vertical Angles: If two triangles meet at a point in a "bowtie" X-shape, the opposite angles at the center are congruent!
  • Given Marks: Match 1 tick with 1 tick, 2 ticks with 2 ticks, and matching angle arcs.
2. The 5 Valid Congruence Theorems (Parts b & c)
SSS: 3 pairs of sides
SAS: 2 sides + included angle
ASA: 2 angles + included side
AAS: 2 angles + non-included side
HL: Right triangles only! Hypotenuse + 1 Leg
3. Watch Out for Traps & Vertex Order!
  • NO SSA (or ASS): 2 sides and a non-included angle is NOT enough info (unless it's a right triangle with HL).
  • NO AAA: 3 pairs of angles only proves similarity, not congruence.
  • Vertex Order Matters: In ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF, A must match D, B must match E, and C must match F!
